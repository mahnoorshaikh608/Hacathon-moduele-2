Chicken Burger

Ingredients

  • Chicken Patty
  • Burger Bun
  • Cheese
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Mayonnaise
  • Ketchup

Cooking Method

Season the chicken patty with spices and cook it until golden and fully cooked. Toast the burger bun and spread mayonnaise and ketchup on it. Add lettuce, tomato, chicken patty and cheese. Finally, place the top bun over it and serve your delicious chicken burger.

Chicken Biryani

Ingredients

  • Basmati Rice
  • Chicken
  • Yogurt
  • Onions
  • Tomatoes
  • Biryani Masala
  • Ginger and Garlic
  • Green Chilies
  • Fresh Coriander
  • Mint Leaves

Cooking Method

Wash and soak the basmati rice. Cook the chicken with onions, tomatoes, yogurt, ginger, garlic and biryani masala until tender. Boil the rice separately until almost cooked. Layer the rice and chicken together with mint, coriander and green chilies. Cover the pot and cook on low heat for 15 to 20 minutes. Mix gently and serve the hot and flavorful chicken biryani.
